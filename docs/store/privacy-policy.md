# Privacy Policy

**App:** Barback
**Last updated:** 7 November 2026

This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **Barback does not collect your personal data. Everything you save stays on your device.**

## Who we are

The app is published by [DEVELOPER NAME]. You can contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

## What the app stores on your device

To work as you expect, the app saves a few things **only on your device**, in the storage of the app or of your browser:

- the drinks you mark as favorites
- the ingredients you tick in your pantry
- the drinks you viewed recently
- your settings (language, measurement units, keep-screen-awake choice)

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it. It is removed when you use "Reset all data" in the app's Settings, when you clear the app's data, or when you uninstall the app.

## What we do not do

- We do not ask you to create an account or to sign in.
- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.
- We do not use analytics, tracking tools, advertising or advertising identifiers.
- We do not use cookies for tracking.
- We do not sell, rent or share your data, because we do not have it.
- The app does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

## Technical data and hosting

The app is made of web technologies. When it opens or checks for updates, its files may be downloaded from our hosting provider, [HOSTING PROVIDER]. Like any web server, the provider may record technical information about the request (for example the IP address and the time) in standard server logs, for security and operation. We do not use these logs to identify you or to profile you. If you use the app offline, nothing is downloaded.

The app store you used to get the app (Google Play or the Apple App Store) may collect its own information about your download and device under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your personal information from the store.

## Children

Barback is **not directed to children under 13**, and it is not meant for minors. It contains cocktail recipes, many of which include alcohol, and it is intended for adults of legal drinking age in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, children included. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will look into it.

## Your rights (GDPR and similar laws)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or a place with similar laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to provide or delete. You can delete everything the app saved by using "Reset all data" in Settings or by uninstalling the app. If you have a question or a request, write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Italy: the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it).

## Security

Your saved information stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Files that the app downloads are delivered over a secure (HTTPS) connection.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if we add accounts), we will update this policy before the change and change the "Last updated" date above.
